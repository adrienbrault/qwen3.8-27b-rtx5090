Withdrawn by R193c below: the differences in this paragraph are a per-boot draw, not the sampler. On that one artifact the sharded sampler is not bitwise equal to the unsharded one. The flag builds a batch sharder and routes logits through the model's local-logits path (each rank still computes its vocab shard for every row, then an all-to-all replaces the 19.9 MB logits all-gather, and the sampler runs on a per-rank row subset); the compiled forward is untouched. One observation does not fit: in R193 a sharded-sampling arm on a fresh compile was bitwise equal to an unsharded arm on another fresh compile. R193c, queued next, boots both modes again on this same artifact and compares each with its twin here, to tell a reproducible numerics change from a non-deterministic sampler.
